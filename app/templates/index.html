How it works

Three steps from a topic to a study habit.

No setup ritual. Tell LearnForge what you are studying and how you learn best — then generate, practise and track.

Create your free account
  1. 01

    Set your learning profile

    Choose your level, preferred language, learning style, goals and the subjects you care about.

  2. 02

    Generate exactly what you need

    Notes, an exam-focused summary, a practical tutorial or a quiz — personalized every time.

  3. 03

    Study, review and improve

    Follow your plan, complete quizzes and let the progress dashboard show you what to revisit.
