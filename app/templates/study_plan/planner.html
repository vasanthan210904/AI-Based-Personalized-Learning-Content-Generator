How the plan is built

  1. Topics are distributedEach topic gets its own study day, then repeats as revision days.
  2. Daily objectivesEvery day has measurable objectives, activities and practice questions.
  3. Revision built inAround 20% of each session is reserved for active recall and review.
  4. Track completionCheck off each day and watch the plan progress update.
